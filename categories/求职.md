## 27. 面试官追问链预测

**Prompt：**
```
Predict the follow-up question chain for my interview answers:

Position: [职位]
My planned answers: [粘贴我要讲的面试回答/故事]
JD keywords: [JD 关键词]
Weak spots in my profile: [如：转行/空窗/大厂背景]

Simulate:
1. For each planned answer: 3 likely follow-up questions an interviewer would ask (probing depth, numbers, edge cases)
2. The "hardest follow-up" per answer — the one testing my claim's validity
3. Behavioral chain: if I use a STAR story, what "why" questions follow (why this approach, why not X, what would you change)
4. Curveballs for my weak spots: 5 questions designed to probe them + strong responses
5. "Question I should ask them" suggestions per round (to show strategic thinking)

Format: Answer → likely follow-ups (ranked by probability) → strongest response for the top one.
```

> 来源：Techpresso AI Academy — 求职面试提示词 2026（追问模拟扩展）
> 用法：面试前预测"追问链"：每个回答后面最可能的 3 个追问+最强应对，重点演练针对弱点的刁钻问题。

## 28. 职业抉择决策树（七问筛选）

**Prompt：**
```
基于你了解的我的职业目标与价值观（[简述]），为我的这个两难抉择生成决策树：

抉择：[如：换工作还是留下 / 转行还是深耕 / 接 offer A 还是 B]

要求：
1. 设计 7 个层层递进的 YES/NO 问题
2. 每个问题基于我的优先级逐步筛选
3. 每个分支的结论要非常具体（如："申请这次内部转岗面试""更新简历""安排一次试岗日"），不要模糊建议
4. 输出格式：可截图的决策树（缩进/箭头），方便以后复用
```
> 📅 2026-08-24
> 来源：Esther Jacobs — 10 Surprising ChatGPT Prompts for Self-Discovery
> 用法：把纠结变成 7 个问题。每一步只答 YES/NO，AI 根据你的价值观逐层筛选，最后落到具体动作而非空泛建议。
