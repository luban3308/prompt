## 1. 合同条款风险审查

**Prompt：**
```
Act as a contracts attorney with experience in [管辖法律/行业].

Review the following contract clause(s) and identify:

Contract type: [合同类型]
Clause(s) to review: [粘贴条款文本]

For each risk identified:
1. Risk level (high/medium/low)
2. Plain English explanation of what it means
3. Why it's problematic for [我方身份]
4. Suggested revision language
5. Negotiation leverage point

Also flag: missing clauses that should be present given the contract type.
```
> 📅 2026-07-28
> 来源：TheLegalPrompts.com — Prompt Engineering for Lawyers 2026
> 用法：合同条款风险审查，含风险评级和修改建议。

## 2. 诉讼案情时间线生成

**Prompt：**
```
Create a litigation chronology from the following facts/documents:

Case type: [案件类型]
Documents/notes: [粘贴文件或笔记]

Organize as a timeline with:
1. Date (or estimated date range)
2. Event description (one sentence)
3. Key players involved
4. Document source reference
5. Legal significance / relevancy

Highlight: statute of limitation dates, contractual deadlines, and any gaps in the timeline that need further investigation.
```
> 📅 2026-07-28
> 来源：Turley Law — AI and the Law in 2026
> 用法：从案件材料自动生成诉讼时间线，便于律师快速把握案情脉络。

## 3. 法律备忘录大纲生成

**Prompt：**
```
Generate a structured legal memorandum outline for:

Legal issue: [法律问题]
Jurisdiction: [管辖法院/地区]
Facts: [关键事实]

Structure:
1. Heading (TO / FROM / DATE / RE)
2. Question Presented (one sentence)
3. Brief Answer (2-3 sentences)
4. Facts (relevant facts only, organized chronologically)
5. Discussion (legal analysis with rules and application)
6. Conclusion

For each section, identify what research is needed and flag any missing information.
```
> 📅 2026-07-28
> 来源：AI Buzz — AI Prompts for Legal Professionals 2026
> 用法：法律备忘录的结构化大纲，节省律师从空白页开始的起草时间。
