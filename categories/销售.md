## 9. 4T 冷邮件框架

**Prompt：**
```
用 4T 框架生成一封简洁、价值驱动的冷邮件和主题行：
1. Trigger 触发点：为什么现在联系（基于对对方角色/团队/公司的具体洞察）；
2. Think 思考：点明与其工作相关的潜在问题；
3. Third-Party Credibility 第三方可信度：用成功数据或社会证明建立信任；
4. Tease/Objection 处理主要异议：消除摩擦，给出低承诺的下一步。
正文控制在 90 词以内，主题行不超过 6 个词。
```
> 来源：Josh Braun (LinkedIn) — 4T cold email prompt
> 用法：先让 AI "想"再"写"，产出比直接要一封邮件高级得多。

## 19. 触发事件冷邮件（Funding/招聘信号）

**Prompt：**
```
Write a trigger-based cold email. The first seller to reach a decision-maker after a trigger event is 5x more likely to win the deal.

Trigger event: [如：公司刚完成 B 轮融资 / 发布新招聘岗位 / CEO 公开提到扩张计划]
Prospect: [姓名/职位]
Company: [公司]
Our product: [产品/服务]
How it helps: [解决什么问题]

Email structure:
1. Reference the specific, verifiable event (show you did homework)
2. Connect the event to a problem we solve
3. One specific benefit, one proof point
4. Low-friction CTA ("Would a 10-min chat make sense?")
Under 100 words, skimmable, no generic compliments.
```
> 来源：Autobound — Cold Email Templates That Get Replies (2026) / SalesHandy
> 用法：信号触发型冷邮件（融资/招聘/扩张）转化率最高（18% vs 泛发 3.4%），关键是引用可验证的具体事件。

## 20. 幽默跟进邮件（未回复）

**Prompt：**
```
Write a concise and professional but funny sales email to a prospect who's been unresponsive.
Use subtle humor — nothing over the top.

Context: [我们之前联系过什么]
Prospect: [职位/公司]
Value: [我们的核心价值]

Rules:
- 3-4 sentences max
- One light joke or witty observation about the silence (e.g., "assuming my last email got lost in the spam folder")
- No emojis, no exclamation marks
- Clear but soft CTA
- Tone: human, not salesy
```
> 来源：Zapier — How to Use ChatGPT for Sales (+ prompt examples)
> 用法：沉默客户的跟进用"轻微自嘲式幽默"破冰，保持专业但像真人，避免施压感。

## 24. 冷邮件主题行（<4 词）

**Prompt：**
```
Give me 10 high-performing cold email subject lines tailored to [角色/行业].

Requirements:
- Under 4 words
- Curiosity-driven but NOT clickbait
- No spam trigger words (free, guaranteed, urgent, etc.)
- Personalized angle when possible ([公司名] or [他们的成就])
- Each with a one-line note on why it works

Rank them by predicted open likelihood.
```
> 来源：Cleverly — ChatGPT Prompts for Cold Email Subject Lines (2026)
> 用法：冷邮件主题行 4 词以内、好奇但不标题党；让 AI 标注每条的工作原理并排序，方便快速选优。
