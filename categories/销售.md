## 1. 销售代表每日活动规划

**Prompt：**
```
Act as a sales manager. Based on my territory and appointments below, build my daily activity plan: [paste appointments + accounts]. Optimize travel routes, block time for prospecting and follow-ups, protect 2 hours for deep selling, and show the plan as a time-blocked schedule.
```
> 📅 2026-08-11
> 来源：SPOTIO — 30+ AI Prompts for Sales Complete Library 2026
> 用法：外勤销售每天开工前跑一次，路线、拜访、拓客时间块一次排好。

## 2. 提案后跟进序列规划

**Prompt：**
```
I just sent a proposal to [client]. Create a value-driven, multi-touch follow-up plan to keep momentum without being pushy: 6 touches over 3 weeks (email, phone, LinkedIn, value-add content), each with a script and a clear next ask. Include a "break-up" email at the end.
```
> 📅 2026-08-11
> 来源：SPOTIO — 30+ AI Prompts for Sales Complete Library 2026
> 用法：报价后最怕冷场，这套多触点跟进序列防止商机无声流失，含最后的"告别邮件"。

## 3. 高频异议应答预演

**Prompt：**
```
List the top 3-5 objections I'm most likely to hear for [product] from [buyer persona], based on: [paste common objections or competitor complaints]. For each: a calm, honest response that acknowledges the concern, reframes it, and moves to a question. Keep each response under 60 words.
```
> 📅 2026-08-11
> 来源：SPOTIO — 30+ AI Prompts for Sales Complete Library 2026
> 用法：拜访前把最常遇到的异议过一遍，用"承认-重构-提问"三步法应答，不再临场发挥。

## 4. 成交后客户交接脚本

**Prompt：**
```
Write a handoff script for moving a newly won client from sales to onboarding/customer success. Include: (1) a kickoff email from the CS manager, (2) the first onboarding call agenda, (3) what the CS team must know about this deal (promises made, timeline, stakeholders). Client context: [paste notes]
```
> 📅 2026-08-11
> 来源：SPOTIO — 30+ AI Prompts for Sales Complete Library 2026
> 用法：成交即交接，把销售过程中承诺过的内容结构化传给客户成功团队，避免落地落差。

## 5. 流失客户赢回邮件

**Prompt：**
```
Write a win-back email sequence (3 emails) for [segment of churned customers]. They stopped using [product] because: [reason if known]. Email 1: acknowledge and add value. Email 2: share what changed / new feature relevant to them. Email 3: make it easy to return with a low-friction offer. Keep each under 120 words.
```
> 📅 2026-08-11
> 来源：SPOTIO — 30+ AI Prompts for Sales Complete Library 2026
> 用法：按"承认-展示变化-降低回归门槛"三封递进，唤醒沉默客户。
