## 6. 销售漏斗阶段诊断

**Prompt：**
```
Diagnose my sales funnel. Stages with current numbers: [paste: leads → qualified → demo → proposal → closed, with counts/conversion rates]. Timeframe: [period]. Identify: (1) the stage with the biggest drop-off and likely causes, (2) the quickest win available (highest impact, lowest effort), (3) what to measure per stage going forward, (4) one experiment to run this week at the weakest stage.
```

## 7. 客户拜访前情报简报

**Prompt：**
```
Build a pre-meeting intelligence brief for my sales call with [company] on [date]. Research points: [paste what I know: person, company, recent news, tech stack, pain signals]. Produce: (1) a 3-bullet summary of why this meeting matters, (2) 3 tailored talking points tied to their situation (not generic), (3) 3 discovery questions ranked by insight value, (4) likely objections and one-line responses, (5) a specific, low-pressure next step to propose at the end.
```

## 8. 年度续约价值回顾话术

**Prompt：**
```
Help me retain [client] at renewal. Their usage: [paste usage data, wins, support tickets]. Original value prop: [what they bought]. Create: (1) a renewal conversation script that leads with the value they actually got (quantified wins, not features), (2) 3 discovery questions to surface new needs and risks, (3) upsell framing if usage shows an adjacent need, (4) a discount ladder: what to offer only if facing churn risk, (5) a written value recap email to send before the call.
```
> 📅 2026-08-16
> 来源：SPOTIO / 综合整理 — 2026 客户成功提示词实践
> 用法：续约前用"价值回顾"而不是"续费请求"开场，显著提升续约率。
