## 13. 副歌记忆点设计

**Prompt：**
```
Design a memorable chorus (副歌) for my song:

Song theme: [主题/情绪]
Genre: [曲风]
Verse content: [主歌内容/情绪铺垫]
Artist style: [风格参考]
Language: [中文/英文]

Design:
1. Hook concept: the core phrase/line that will be remembered (3 options, varying in catchiness level)
2. Melodic contour: how the chorus melody should move vs. verse (range, step vs. leap, repetition pattern)
3. Rhythmic signature: what makes it stick (repetition, syncopation, rhythmic motif)
4. Lyrical technique: rhyme scheme, repetition strategy, emotional peak placement
5. Production notes: where the chorus sits dynamically (instrumentation lift, backing vocals)
6. "Hum test": would someone hum it after one listen? — evaluate each hook option

Deliver: 3 chorus options with full lyrics + production notes for each.
```

> 来源：Suno 音乐提示词系列 2026（副歌设计扩展）
> 用法：副歌=记忆点工程：钩子句 3 选 1、旋律走向、节奏动机、制作动态全链路设计，最后做"哼唱测试"。
