## 1. Suno AI 音乐生成提示词——流行摇滚

**Prompt：**
```
Generate a song with the following specifications:

Genre: Pop-rock
Tempo: 110 BPM
Key: C Major
Mood: Uplifting, anthemic
Theme: Overcoming self-doubt and finding inner strength
Structure: Intro → Verse 1 → Chorus → Verse 2 → Chorus → Bridge → Final Chorus → Outro
Instruments: Electric guitar (crunchy), driving drums, bass, layered synths in chorus
Vocal style: Confident male tenor, slight rasp in chorus

[Lyrics - optional: paste your lyrics here]
```

## 2. 歌词创作助手

**Prompt：**
```
Write lyrics for a [genre] song about [theme/topic].

The lyrics should:
- Follow a standard song structure: Verse-Chorus-Verse-Chorus-Bridge-Chorus
- Include a memorable hook/chorus (4-6 lines)
- Use concrete imagery and specific details (avoid clichés)
- Have a consistent rhyme scheme (AABB or ABAB in verses)
- Tell a story or convey a clear emotional arc

Title suggestion: [optional]

Also provide: guidance on syllable count per line for melody fitting.
```
> 📅 2026-07-28
> 来源：Mubert — How to Use AI to Write a Song in 2026
> 用法：AI 歌词创作，含结构、韵律和情感弧线指导。
