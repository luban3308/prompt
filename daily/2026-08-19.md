## 15. AI 漂移检测润色

**Prompt：**
```
Read the Voice Card in my CLAUDE.md (under "## Writing Voice"). Then read the draft at [Your Vault]/[article].md. Run these 7 checks against both:

- VOICE DRIFT CHECK: Compare sentence patterns, rhythm, and tone against the Voice Card. Quote drifted sentences and explain what my Voice Card says I would write.
- EVALUATE INTENSIFIERS: Check every intensifier. Keep the word only when removing it changes meaning or contrast.
- REMOVE EM DASHES: Try a period first, then a colon, comma, or rewrite. Keep the source at zero prose em dashes.
- REMOVE FORMULAIC CONTRASTS: State the supported point directly.
- APPLY THE BANNED-LANGUAGE LIST: Report every match before editing.
- ADD SPECIFICITY: "hundreds" → "300+", "takes some time" → "20 hours". Name every vague "something".
- PUBLISHED COMPARISON: Find one sentence least like something I'd publish and one most like me. Explain why.

For each change, note what was changed and why. Show proposed edits and wait for approval before applying.
```
> 来源：Build to Launch — 15 Best Claude Code Prompts That Earn Me 30 Hours a Week
> 用法：发布前的最后一道编辑关卡，专治 AI 长期写作导致的"声音漂移"；约 90 秒一篇，能抓出 8-12 处人工难发现的问题。
