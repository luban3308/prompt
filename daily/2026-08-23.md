## 1. 会议效率评分与改进建议

**Prompt：**
```
Review this meeting transcript/agenda and score its effectiveness:

[粘贴会议记录或议程]

Score (1-10) and critique these dimensions:
1. Purpose clarity — was the meeting's goal explicit?
2. Time utilization — time spent vs. value delivered per agenda item
3. Decision velocity — were decisions made, or just discussed?
4. Action item quality — are owners, deadlines, and next steps clear?
5. Participation balance — did key voices dominate or get excluded?

For each dimension: give a score, 1-2 sentence justification, and one concrete improvement tactic for the next meeting.
End with a "before next meeting" checklist (pre-reads, decision owners, time budget).
```

> 用法：让 AI 对会议进行多维评分，把模糊的"会开得不好"变成可执行的改进清单，每次会后花 2 分钟自检。
