## 1. 个人董事会年度作战规划

**Prompt：**
```
Act as my personal board of directors. Help me plan [year/quarter].
1. Define what winning means for me right now (specific, measurable)
2. Audit my money and time: where did they actually go last month?
3. Identify my unfair advantages (skills, assets, network, position)
4. Lock in 3 priorities and force real trade-offs — what am I willing to give up?
5. Build a quarter-by-quarter operating plan with one scoreboard metric per priority

No motivation, no fluff — just clarity, leverage, and a strategic plan I can follow.
```
> 来源：Nick Sadler 2026 年度规划提示词（Instagram 病毒传播）
> 用法：把 ChatGPT 变成你的"个人董事会"，做季度/年度战略规划，强制做取舍，避免空喊目标。

## 2. 人工截止日期创造器

**Prompt：**
```
I need to complete [task] by [final deadline]. I tend to procrastinate on open-ended work.

- Break the task into 5-7 milestones
- Assign each milestone a specific date BEFORE the real deadline (with buffer)

## 37. 成功简报草稿批评修订循环

**Prompt：**
```
Run this loop for [task]:
1. Success Brief — define the task, success criteria, audience, and constraints in 5 bullet points
2. Draft — produce the deliverable
3. Critique — evaluate the draft against the success criteria; list every failure and weak point honestly
4. Revise — fix the failures and produce the final version
